P2P

Personal experiments with encrypted tunnels and connectivity between devices.

WireGuard tunnels

I configured WireGuard tunnels between macOS peers, including public keys, tunnel addresses, endpoints, and handshake checks. I investigated address allocation, IP forwarding, NAT, and full-tunnel routing, and troubleshot Windows adapter initialization.

A successful handshake and working packet forwarding are separate checks: routing and forwarding still need to be verified.

Tailscale connectivity

I used Tailscale to test connectivity and file transfer between devices on different networks, and explored relay and port-forwarding designs for home-network reachability.
